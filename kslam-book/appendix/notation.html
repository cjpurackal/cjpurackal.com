Transforms

pose convention A transform carries two frame subscripts, read right to left: Tab​ maps coordinates expressed in Fb​ into Fa​:

xa​=Tab​xb​=Rab​xb​+tab​.

Adjacent subscripts cancel when composing, which makes errors easy to spot:

Tac​=Tab​Tbc​,Tab−1​=Tba​.

SymbolMeaningkornia-slam
Tcw​camera pose, world-to-camerapose_world_to_cam: Pose3d

Tbc​camera-to-body extrinsiccamera_to_body: Option<Pose3d> (T_BC)

kornia-slam stores Tcw​ for every frame and keyframe, as ORB-SLAM does. The camera's position in the world is therefore twc​=−Rcw⊤​tcw​, not tcw​.
